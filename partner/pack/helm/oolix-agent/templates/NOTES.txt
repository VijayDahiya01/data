The Oolix Agent is starting in namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.

     kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} logs deploy/{{ include "oolix-agent.name" . }} | grep setup_password

     kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward deploy/{{ include "oolix-agent.name" . }} 8083:8083

   then browse to http://localhost:8083 and follow the six steps.

3. Your backend asks for ad decisions at:

     http://{{ include "oolix-agent.name" . }}.{{ .Release.Namespace }}.svc:8082/private/v1/ad-decision
